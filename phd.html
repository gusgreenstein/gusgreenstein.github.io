Want to do a PhD?

Interested in PhD opportunities related to environmental governance?

Like many academics - especially those in Europe - I constantly receive email ads for interesting PhD opportunities related to environmental governance. However, it can sometimes be difficult to remember all the former students, and others, who have let me know they are interested in these kinds of opportunities. And I'm sure there are many more people out there who'd like to be aware of these positions but would probably not become aware of them unless an academic forwarded them the ad.

To help address this, I started a listserv where I can easily share these with those who are interested.

If you'd like to be on the listserv, please e-mail me with the email address you'd like to be included!
